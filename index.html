What Vedank Numbers Does

Birth Grid Analysis

Enter any birth date and instantly see the complete 3×3 Vedic numerology grid with number placements, base number, and destiny number.

Dasha Periods

View Main, Antar, Pratyantar and Daily dasha periods. Navigate through time with colour-coded numbers showing favourable and cautious phases.

Observations & Yogs

The app identifies specific patterns in your grid. Green chips mark favourable yogs. Red chips highlight areas needing awareness.

Event Planning

Analyse future dates for events. Compare two dates side by side with full dasha details to pick the most favourable one.

Date Comparison

Select two dates, see grids and 10-year dasha outlook for each, with colour-coded MD and AD numbers indicating grid matches.

Share & Collaborate

Share comparison cards as images via WhatsApp, email, or any channel. Beautiful cards with grids, dashas, and branding included.

How It Works

1

Enter a Date of Birth

Type a name and birth date, or pick from saved entries. The date can be from the past or future.

2

See Your Grid

The app computes your unique 3×3 grid, places your numbers, identifies yogs, and shows your destiny interpretation.

3

Explore Dashas

Navigate through Main, Antar, Pratyantar and Daily dasha periods. See how different time periods affect your grid.

Vedank Numbers uses a custom Vedic numerology system — not Lo Shu, not Western numerology. The 3×3 grid, dasha computation, and yog classification follow traditional Vedic principles adapted for modern use.
