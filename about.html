The Numerology System

Vedank Numbers uses a custom Vedic numerology system. It is not Lo Shu. It is not Western numerology. The 3×3 grid layout, digit placement rules, dasha weight distribution, and yog detection follow a specific methodology developed through years of practice and consultation.

The app's algorithm computes accurately but is not a substitute for professional consultation. For confirmed readings and personalised guidance, consult a reputed Numerologist.
