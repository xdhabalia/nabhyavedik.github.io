Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 August 2026

About Vedank Numbers

Vedank Numbers is a Vedic Numerology application developed by Nabhya Vedik. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Data We Collect

Vedank Numbers does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers. The app operates entirely offline.

The only data the app stores is:

  • Names and dates of birth you enter for numerology computation
  • Your last four birth date entries and one event plan entry for convenience

This data is stored locally on your device only. It is never sent to any server, cloud service, or third party.

Data Sharing

When you use the Share feature, the app creates an image of the comparison card and opens your device's native share sheet. The content you share is sent directly from your device to your chosen app (WhatsApp, email, etc.). Vedank Numbers does not intercept, store, or process any shared content.

Third-Party Services

Vedank Numbers does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, tracking, or crash reporting services. No SDKs from Google Analytics, Facebook, or any other service are included.

Permissions

The app does not request any special device permissions. It does not access your contacts, camera, microphone, location, or files.

Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. The app contains no inappropriate content and is suitable for all ages.

Data Deletion

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you can delete it at any time by:

  • Clearing the app data from your device Settings
  • Uninstalling the app

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
