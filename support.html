Support & FAQ

What is Vedank Numbers?▼
Vedank Numbers is a free Vedic Numerology app that computes your birth grid, dasha periods, and classified observations from any date of birth. It uses a custom Vedic system — not Lo Shu, not Western numerology.
Is the app free?▼
Yes, completely free. No in-app purchases, no ads, no hidden costs. The app works fully offline — no internet needed after installation.
Does the app collect my data?▼
No. All data stays on your device. Nothing is sent to any server. Read our complete Privacy Policy for details.
How accurate is this numerology system?▼
The app computes grids and dashas algorithmically based on traditional Vedic numerology principles. However, numerology is an interpretive art. We recommend using the app as a reference tool and consulting a reputed Numerologist for confirmed readings and personal guidance.
What is the difference between this and Lo Shu grid?▼
Vedank Numbers uses a different grid layout [3,1,9 / 6,7,5 / 2,8,4] and a distinct set of rules for digit placement, dasha computation, and yog classification. It follows Vedic numerology traditions, not Chinese Lo Shu numerology.
Can I use a future date of birth?▼
Yes. Enter any future date in Birth Date mode to see the grid. However, dashas will only be shown from the entered date forward — no dasha periods are computed before the birth date.
How does the date comparison work?▼
In the Plan Future Event mode, toggle two dates using the switches. Tap "Compare the Dates" to see both grids side by side with 10 years of Main and Antar dasha numbers, colour-coded to show grid matches, destiny alignment, and cautious numbers.
What do the green and red chips mean?▼
Green chips are favourable observations (yogs) — strengths created by your birth date numbers. Red chips are areas that need awareness, where you can shape your karma through conscious effort.
How do I share a comparison?▼
On the Date Comparison screen, tap the Share button. The app generates an image card with both grids, dasha details, and branding. Your phone's native share sheet opens — choose WhatsApp, email, or any app to share.
Can I save my entries?▼
Yes. The app automatically saves your last 4 birth date entries and 1 event plan entry on your device. Use the dropdown on the data entry screen to reload any saved entry.
